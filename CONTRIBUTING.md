# Contributing

This initial edition accepts documentation corrections, verified provenance references and licensing evidence. Keep evidence attributable to its exact version and distinguish a declared license from a complete applicable notice or output exception. Do not upload game resources, binaries, saves, keys or raw private logs.

Original documentation contributions use the MIT terms of LICENSE; preserve authorship and rights of third-party quotations or references. Engine code contributions are deferred until its licensing scope is published. Do not claim AI ownership of upstream work or remove existing authors' credits.
