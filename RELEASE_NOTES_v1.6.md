# Reloaded-NX 1.6 — history and publication notes

Prepared 2026-11-20 (America/Santiago).

Version 1.6 is the existing stable Nintendo Switch runner. Its cumulative behavior includes configurable controls, Mega/battle, pointer/right-stick mapping, right Joy-Con SL/SR, PC/map/Fly, Safari and casino support. The maintainer reports successful operation on Nintendo Switch. This is a maintainer report, not a new comprehensive regression run.

The stable development base is `2aaeedfbbd10f317e7b5bdb1ac4d241c110c313c` on the private `codex/reloaded-nx` line; cumulative 1.5 was preserved at `5e25a699dd0de91cb19f17de9b9f101c00b309d7`.
